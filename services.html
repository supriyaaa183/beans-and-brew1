OUR BREWING EXPERIENCE

The Art of Brewing Coffee

Four brewing methods. Four unique coffee experiences.

1. V60 Pour Over

A delicate brewing technique that produces clean, bright coffee with balanced flavours and beautiful aromas.

2. Cold Brew

Slowly steeped in cold water using a specialised brewing device, creating a smooth, refreshing coffee.

3. French Press

A traditional immersion brewing method delivering rich body, bold flavours and wonderful aromas.

4. Espresso

A concentrated coffee extracted under pressure, delivering intense flavour and a beautiful golden crema.
